# Geovana Neves

Aerodynamics and flight dynamics engineer. Graduate student at Instituto Tecnologico de Aeronautica (ITA), researching propeller airframe aerodynamic interaction in collaboration with TU Delft.

Curiosity is common. Contribution is curiosity with a method.

## What I publish here

- [from-curiosity-to-wisdom](https://github.com/nevesgeovana/from-curiosity-to-wisdom): didactic materials on research craft and AI assisted research methodology. The first material, From Curiosity to Contribution, is a complete method for doing rigorous research with an AI assistant, demonstrated end to end on a real published paper: an 89 page deck, a mobile cheat sheet, and a cloneable workspace template.
